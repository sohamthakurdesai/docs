## Query: 

```yaml
Was Tilak calling Mrs. Besant पुतना मावशी in a good taste?
```

## Answer

No, the context indicates that the comparison of Mrs. Besant to Putana was not made in a good taste. The text explains that Putana was a "treacherous (female) demon employed to entrap and kill Shri Krishna by his enemies" (page 398). The comparison was used to describe Mrs. Besant's actions, stating that "Mrs. Besant went to England with the deliberate object of pushing her own views in preference to those of the Congress and thus establishing her supremacy in matters political. In other words, she desired to kill the Congress, if we may so call it, by pretending to speak for the nation" (page 398).

The text further justifies this comparison as "the best Puranic illustration to describe her efforts and conduct in this behalf" (page 398). In the Marathi text, it is mentioned that Mr. Khaparde "अखेरीस पूतना- मावशीहि बनविलें" (finally made her Putana-Mavshi) and this was justified by the principle of 'tit for tat' ("जशास तसें' म्हणून जं कर्मयोगी वेदान्ताचें सूत्र आहे तें या ठिकाणीं लागू केलें पाहिजे") (page 501). This clearly shows the comparison was intended as a severe criticism of her perceived treacherous actions against the Congress.

## Analysis

While the retrieval is more or less right. It misses the point that Tilak didn't call Mrs. Besant Putana mavshi, Mr. Khaparde did. The answer should have explicitelty mentioned that.
